Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 27, 2026

JellyDrop ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we assign you a randomly generated username. You may optionally provide an email address for account recovery purposes.

User Content: We collect the posts, comments, and chat messages you create within the app.

Usage Data: We may collect basic usage information such as app interactions to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the app
  • Enable communication between users
  • Send verification codes to your email (if provided)
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our service

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not use any third-party analytics or advertising services. Your posts and comments are visible to other users of the app. Chat messages are only visible to the participants of the conversation.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

Posts are automatically removed from the public feed after 72 hours but remain accessible on your profile. You may delete your posts at any time. Account data is retained until you choose to delete your account.

When you delete your account, your profile is deactivated and you will no longer be able to log in or use the app. Your previously created posts, comments, and likes will remain visible to other users but will no longer be associated with an active account. Other users will not be able to start new conversations with you.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your posts and comments
  • Delete your account directly from the app via Settings > Delete Account
  • Opt out of providing an email address

7. Children's Privacy

JellyDrop is not intended for children under 4 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 4.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: jellydrop.app@gmail.com
